# Pork Stroganoff
---
**America's Test Kitchen**

Purchased  | Pantry Items
-----------  | ------------
1/2 cup sour cream | 2oz white wine
Green onions.      | 4 cups chicken broth
8 oz egg noodles.  | 2 tsp tomato paste
3 garlic cloves.   | 2 + 1 tbs butter
4 tsp sage.        | 2 + 1 tbs flour
1 onion.           | 
12 oz mushrooms.   |
1 small pork tenderloin in 1/4" slices |
4 oz bacon in 1/4" slices              |




## Timeline (60 min total)
* 10 min prep
* 15 min cook bacon and pork
* 15 min cook onions
* 10 min cook pasta
* 10 min assemble




## Steps

1. **Brown** bacon and remove leaving fat
2. **Toss** pork in 2tbs flour, salt and pepper
3. Add **1 tbs butter** and cook pork in 1 layer 2 minutes
4. Toss pork and cook an additional 1 minute
5. **Transfer** to bacon leaving behind oil
6. Add **2 tbs butter** and cook onion and mushrooms
7. **Toss** in 1tbs flour, tomato paste, sage, garlic
8. With wine **deglaze** pan then add **broth**
9. Add **noodles** and cook 9 minutes
10. Mix in **pork and bacon** to rehead
11. **Off-head** mix in sour cream and green onions
12. Season with salt and pepper
