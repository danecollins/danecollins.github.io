# Sausage Rigatoni
---
**Cook's Illustrated**

Purchased  | Pantry Items
-----------  | ------------
7 oz rigatoni | 2 oz white wine
8 oz sausage  | 3 oz broth
1 shallot     | 3/4 oz parmesan
4 oz cream    |



## Timeline (35 min total)

* 10 min prep
* 20 min cook sausage and shallot and pasta
* 5 min to assemble



## Steps

1. **Brown** sausage, place in bowl
2. Cook noodles **12-13 minutes**
2. Brown **shallot**
3. Add butter and /2 tsp/ flour and brown to **roux**
4. Mix in **wine** and cook down
5. Add **cream, broth** and reduce
6. Add sausage and heat
7. **Off heat** mix in cheese
8. Add noodles, salt and pepper
