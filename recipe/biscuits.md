# Buttermilk Biscuits
---
**America's Test Kitchen**

Purchased    | Pantry Items
-----------  | ------------
4 tbs vegetable shortening | 8 tbs unsalted butter
1 1/4 cup buttermilk | 425g all-purpose flour
                     | 1 tbs sugar
                     | 1 tbs baking powder
                     | 1 tsp salt
                     | 1/2 tsp baking soda

## Timeline (60 min total)

* 30 min to refrigerate butter
* 15 min to mix
* 15 min to bake


## Steps

1. **Refrigerate** butter and shortening for 30 minutes
2. Pulse **dry ingredients** in food processor
3. Add **butter and shortening** and pulse to combine
4. **Transfer** mixture to a bowl
5. Add **buttermilk** and mix
6. **Kneed** 8-10 times
7. **Roll** out 3/4" thick
8. Cut rolls and place on **parchment** lined sheet
9. Bake for **5 minutes** in **450 degree** preheated oven
10. **Rotate pan**, lower temp to 400 degrees
11. Bake for additional **10 minutes**
