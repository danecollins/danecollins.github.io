# Ginger Hoisin Shrimp
---
**Cook's Illustrated**

Purchased           | Pantry Items
-----------         | ------------
1 lb shrimp         | 2 tbs hoisin
2 tbs grated ginger | 1 tbs rice vinegar
3 green onions      | 2 tbs soy sauce
7 oz noodles        | Reserved pasta water
                    | 1 tsp cornstarch
                    
              


## Timeline (35 min total)
* 15 min shrimp prep
* 15 min cook pasta
* 5 min to finish




## Steps

1. **Toss** shrimp in sugar/salt/pepper mix
2. Cook **pasta** for 10 minutes
2. **Fry** shrimp starting from cold pan
3. **Mix** all ingredients and add to shrimp
4. Add **reserved water** to desired consistency
4. Add **green onions**



## Notes
* Might be good with chicken
