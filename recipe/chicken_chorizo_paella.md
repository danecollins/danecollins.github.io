# Skillet Chicken Chorizo Paella
---
**Food & Wine**

Purchased     | Pantry Items
-----------   | ------------
6 oz chorizo  | Saffron
1 lb chicken  | 1.5 cups white rice
Green onions  | 1 cup peas
1 small onion | 3 cups chicken broth
1 can diced tomatoes | 1/2 tsp Paprika
                     | 2 cloves garlic



## Timeline (70 min total)
* 20 min cook chicken and chorizo
* 20 min cook vegetables
* 15 min simmer with rice
* 15 min to finish and rest




## Steps

1. Soak crubled saffron in 1/4 cup water
2. Cook **chorizo** in oil and remove leaving oil
3. **Coat** chicken with salt and pepper
3. Cook **chicken** and remove leaving oil
4. Brown **onion** 
5. Add **saffron and paprika** at end
5. Add **tomatoes** and **rice** and cook out liquid
6. Bring broth to **boil**
7. Add in **chicken** and **chorizo**, reduce heat
8. **Simmer covered** for 15 minutes
9. Add **peas** and cook for a few minutes
10. Add **green onions**
11. Let **stand** until remaining liquid absorbed
