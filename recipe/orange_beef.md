# Orange Beef with Green Beans
---
**Adapted from Cook's Illustrated**

Purchased        | Pantry Items
-----------      | ------------
1 lb flank steak | 2 tsp orange zest
8 oz green beans | 3 oz orange juice
green onions     | 2 oz soy sauce
                 | 2 garlic cloves
                 | 1 tbs honey
                 | 1/4 tsp pepper flakes
                 | 1 tsp garlic chili sauce



## Steps

1. **Mix** all pantry ingredients
2. Cook **beef** in 1/4 cup or sauce
3. **Reduce** until dry and sear beef
4. Set **aside** beef
5. Cook **green beans** in remaining sauce
6. Mix **beef** into beans
7. Add **green onions**
